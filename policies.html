STORE & ENQUIRY POLICIES.

This website is currently a product catalogue and availability-enquiry service. Payment collection, online checkout and website-arranged delivery have not been activated.

1. Catalogue and pricing

Retail prices are displayed in INR. Prices, variants and product availability are subject to confirmation before a sale. Wholesale trade terms are shared privately after business enquiries. Product images may be manufacturer reference images; confirm current packaging, label, ingredients and expiry details with us before purchasing.

2. Shopping bag and enquiries

Adding products to your bag does not reserve stock, place an order or create an invoice. When you choose “Send WhatsApp Enquiry”, your browser opens a WhatsApp message containing your selected items and the contact details you entered. Sending the message is voluntary and an enquiry does not confirm a sale.

3. Payment and delivery

No online payments are accepted on this website. No delivery fee is collected and online shipping cannot be booked here. If any separate offline purchase is discussed, request written confirmation of the product, price, payment method, delivery terms and business details before paying.

4. Returns, cancellations and refunds

As no website orders or payments are processed at present, there are no website purchases to cancel or refund. Terms applicable to a future online checkout will be published before that service is activated. For any separate direct transaction, discuss product condition, returns and cancellation terms with us before purchasing; applicable consumer rights remain unaffected.

5. Privacy and local storage

This static site does not create customer accounts or store enquiry messages on a server. The shopping bag is stored locally in your browser so it can be restored. Your name and phone number are used to compose a WhatsApp enquiry; they are not automatically sent to us until you send the message. WhatsApp processes communications under its own privacy terms.

6. Contact and corrections

For the latest pack photographs, ingredient information, product availability or corrections to catalogue details, contact Max Volume Nutrition through WhatsApp or visit our contact page. Location: Chennai, Tamil Nadu, India.
