FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Can I purchase directly through this website?

Online payment and delivery are currently disabled. The catalogue and cart are available for product discovery and WhatsApp availability enquiries only. An enquiry is not a confirmed order.

Are prices shown retail or wholesale?

All publicly displayed product prices are retail customer prices. Wholesale quotations are shared privately after business review.

How do I verify a product label or ingredients?

Request current front, back and ingredients-panel photographs before purchase. Product formulations and packaging may change.

What are the delivery, cancellation and refund terms?

Website delivery and payment services are not yet active. Shipping, cancellations and refunds for any future online orders will be published before online checkout is enabled. For direct offline transactions, confirm the applicable terms with our team before payment.

How are enquiry details used?

Information you voluntarily provide in a WhatsApp enquiry is used to respond to your product or dealer request. WhatsApp processes messages under its own terms. This static website does not operate a customer-account database or payment system.
