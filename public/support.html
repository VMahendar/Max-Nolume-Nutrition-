QUESTIONS & ANSWERS

Everything you need to know.

01 / HELP WITH BUYING

Orders & stock

How can I place an order?

Add products to your bag and send the order enquiry on WhatsApp. Our team confirms current stock and the next steps. The website does not currently take online payment.

Will I receive a confirmation automatically?

No. An enquiry is not a paid or accepted order. Our team confirms availability and order details with you directly.

How are delivery charges and timelines arranged?

Shipping is not automatically booked through this website. Contact our team to confirm serviceability, costs and delivery arrangements before paying.

02 / VERIFY YOUR PRODUCTS

Product authenticity

How can I check my supplement pack?

Check manufacturer and importer details, the batch/lot number, expiry date, tamper seals and any verification method stated on the actual brand label. If uncertain, contact our team with photographs of the front, back, batch and seal. Manufacturer-specific authentication methods may vary.

Can I view your official distributor documentation?

Yes. You can view our Unicorn Pro Series Authorized Distributor certificate above. The document does not by itself establish exclusive territory rights.

03 / MANAGE YOUR PROFILE

Account assistance

Where do I register as a customer?

Use the My Account page to create a retail customer login and view eligible order history.

Where do I manage reminder and festival updates?

Sign in to My Account and visit Communication Preferences. You can choose 15-day, 30-day or no reminders; select festival updates and unsubscribe at any time. Marketing sending is not yet automated.

What if I cannot access my account?

Contact our customer support team. Do not send passwords, OTPs or financial credentials through WhatsApp or email.

04 / DEALER ASSISTANCE

Wholesale partners

How do gyms and supplement shops register?

Use the separate Wholesale Account portal with your business name, business type, contact, location, PIN code and GSTIN if registered.

When can I see wholesale prices?

After an administrator approves your business account. Pending or unverified accounts only see ordinary retail prices. Bulk quantities and fulfilment are confirmed privately.
