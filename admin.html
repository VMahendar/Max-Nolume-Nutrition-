Automated WhatsApp messages

Reminders follow each customer's 15/30-day preference, measured from their latest paid order, consent or manual reminder. Active retail deals and scheduled brand updates go only to customers who selected those topics and WhatsApp. Maximum one accepted message per customer in 24 hours, between 9 am and 8 pm IST.

Checking setup…

Each entry needs a unique id, title, text, timezone-aware startsAt/endsAt, and enabled. Saving an enabled announcement schedules it once delivery is configured.

Provider-accepted means accepted for delivery, not confirmed delivered. Unknown outcomes are held for reconciliation and are never automatically resent. Account preferences and signed unsubscribe links cancel pending messages.

Secure WhatsApp setup in Render

Live delivery needs production mode, MARKETING_AUTOMATION_ENABLED=true, MARKETING_PUBLIC_ORIGIN, a random MARKETING_LINK_SECRET (32+ characters), WHATSAPP_ACCESS_TOKEN, WHATSAPP_PHONE_NUMBER_ID, WHATSAPP_GRAPH_VERSION, WHATSAPP_MARKETING_TEMPLATE and WHATSAPP_TEMPLATE_LANGUAGE. See docs/MARKETING_AUTOMATION.md. Set secrets in Render environment settings, never in this form.
